# arabicR 0.1.0.9000 — Alpha 1

Initial alpha release, tagged `v0.1.0-alpha.1` on GitHub.

* Arabic Unicode shaping and bidirectional text through ragg and textshaping.
* Cairo PNG and PDF graphics devices.
* Noto Sans Arabic font checks and right-aligned grid text helpers.
* ggplot2 theme with Arabic fonts and title alignment.
* Arabic and English examples, rendered PNG/PDF samples, and automated tests.

This is an experimental alpha: function signatures may change. Font installation
is required. Cairo support depends on the R build and operating system. Axis,
factor, and table column ordering remain under the user's control.
